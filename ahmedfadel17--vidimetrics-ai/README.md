> Tome by [AhmedFadel17](https://github.com/AhmedFadel17/VidiMetrics.AI) — distributed by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

VidiMetrics is an AI-driven video content orchestration and story engine backend built on .NET. It streamlines the creation of cinematic shows and multi-media assets by managing high-end generative workflows (AI image/video generation, voiceovers, and rendering) paired with a precise credit cost tracking and subscription ecosystem.
