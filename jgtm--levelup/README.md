# LevelUp

> Source: [JGtm/LevelUp](https://github.com/JGtm/LevelUp). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Halo 5 & Halo Infinite stats dashboard: match history, rends, medals & commendations, medias, sessions & teammates. Powered by Go, DuckDB, React, and ECharts. Supports friends groups, multi-language (FR/EN so far) and includes internal TrueSkill2 rating.
